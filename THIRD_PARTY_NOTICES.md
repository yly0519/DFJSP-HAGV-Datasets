# Third-party data attribution

The ten files under `data/reference/brandimarte` reproduce the exact benchmark inputs used in the formal tests. They were obtained from the Brandimarte collection in https://github.com/SchedulingLab/fjsp-instances. The corresponding original publication is:

Brandimarte, P. (1993). Routing and scheduling in a flexible job shop by tabu search. Annals of Operations Research, 41(3), 157–183. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02023073
